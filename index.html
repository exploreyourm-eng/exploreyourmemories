A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Can we book only photography or only event planning?

Yes. Enquire about either service separately, or request a combined photography and event planning proposal.

Do you help with destination weddings?

We can discuss venue coordination, accommodation, hospitality and event arrangements based on your chosen location, dates and requirements.

How much does event planning cost?

Event costs depend on the venue, guest count, number of functions, décor and services. Share your requirements for a personalised quote.

How do we check availability?

Send your date and location through the enquiry form or call us. The team will confirm availability and discuss the next steps.
